Where do these keys come from?
  1. Create a free account at developer.ebay.com and make an application keyset (Production).
  2. Copy the App ID (Client ID) and Cert ID (Client Secret) into the fields above.
  3. Deploy the included worker.js to Cloudflare Workers (free) and paste its URL as the CORS proxy — browsers can't call eBay directly. Full steps are in the README.
